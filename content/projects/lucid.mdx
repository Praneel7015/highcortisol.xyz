lucid isn't a homepage, it's a place. one continuous scroll walks the camera
down a single path through five chambers: threshold, arrival, the drift, the
archive, the waking. one crystal carries you through it, fractured and dim at
the top and whole and warm by the end.

built for the **3d websites hackathon**. it's **low cortisol** to use: you
just scroll, and nothing asks you to click.
