indian families get dozens of bank, upi and biller messages a week, and a few
of them are fake "kyc pending" collect requests or "your power gets cut
tonight" calls. guardianloop reads all of them. it spots the scams before
anyone pays, tracks bills and quiet subscription price rises, and stays silent
unless there's a real decision to make.

built with the strands agents sdk on amazon bedrock for the
**aws agents for humans hackathon**, everyday agents track. **low cortisol**
is the point: the agent watches the sms inbox so you don't have to.
