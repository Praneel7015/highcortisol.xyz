the same `payment.failed` event can mean four different things. triage works
out which one before it acts. insufficient funds gets a quiet retry after
payday, a bank outage gets nothing, a revoked mandate gets one payment link,
and a cancellation is left alone. a policy graph picks the action, stop rules
veto it, and the llm only writes the copy. it never moves money.

built for the **razorpay ai buildathon 2026**, track 03: ai revenue recovery.
on the 168-case batch, naive dunning recovers more rupees but breaks 157 rules.
triage breaks zero, and every rupee it recovers can be booked. **high cortisol**:
a buildathon deadline, live razorpay payment links, and webhooks on ngrok.
