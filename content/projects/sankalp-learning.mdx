_sankalp_ means **resolve**. the gap between deciding to learn something and
actually learning it is where most plans die. sankalp learning lives in that
gap: it takes the resolve you already have and turns it into a plan that
survives contact with a normal, busy week.

**low cortisol** edtech — no leaderboards screaming at you, no guilt loops. just
a calm path from _i should_ to _i did_, paced for a human who has a life.

## VITALS

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **turns** — a resolve → a plan you keep
- **anti-feature** — no guilt, no streaks
