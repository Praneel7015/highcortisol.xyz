studypulse is a study coach for students. log hours across study, projects,
classes and everything else, set weekly goals, paste in your screen-time, and
once a week amazon bedrock (nova) reads it back to you as a short coaching note.
no network? a local fallback writes the note instead.

built for the aws builder center **build a productivity app** weekend
challenge. **low cortisol** on purpose: a focus timer, a few progress bars, and
one weekly check-in instead of a dashboard that nags you every hour.

## VITALS

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **built for** — aws builder center · productivity weekend challenge
- **stack** — amplify → bedrock nova, with a local fallback
- **anti-feature** — no streaks, one coach note a week
