flowforge reads a sentence and ships a pipeline. you describe the build you
want — _"lint, test, containerize, deploy to staging on green"_ — and it
assembles the CI/CD graph, wires the secrets, and opens the pull request. no
YAML archaeology, no copy-pasting someone else's `.github/workflows`.

this is **high cortisol** work. the deadline was the demo, the demo was in an
hour, and the pitch was: _what if the boring part of shipping took a prompt
instead of an afternoon?_ it turns out the boring part is most of the part.

in october it came back for a second pass and moved to its own subdomain on
this site. it gained a bidirectional dag compiler, groq as the default model,
and vertex ai, bedrock and openai-compatible providers. ci, codeql, dependabot
and coderabbit are now wired in, and a full coderabbit audit closed 28 findings
across more than thirty merged prs.
