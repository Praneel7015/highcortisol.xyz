opportun-ai-t is a single-user career agent. at 08:00 IST an eventbridge
schedule wakes a lambda, bedrock nova scores the day's jobs against your
profile, dynamodb keeps the results, ses mails you a briefing, and a next.js
control center shows what the agent did and why.

built for the aws builder center **weekend agent challenge**. it's
**low cortisol** because the agent does the refreshing for you. you read one email
over coffee and close the tab.

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **built for** — aws builder center · weekend agent challenge
- **cadence** — one briefing a day, 08:00 ist
