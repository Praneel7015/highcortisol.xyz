_namma_ means **ours**. namma city is a civic companion — the small, patient
layer between you and the place you actually live. where's the nearest thing
that's open, who do you call when the thing is broken, what is the city quietly
trying to tell you. built at the GoogleX hackathon over a long calm weekend.

this is **low cortisol** work: software that lowers the resting heart rate of a
whole neighbourhood. it doesn't ask for much. it just wants local life to be a
little less friction.
