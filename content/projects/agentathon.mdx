agentathon is a swarm. you hand it a task and it spins up a crowd of autonomous
agents that plan, split, argue, and converge — racing to be done before you've
finished reading the sentence describing them. (you're still reading. they're
probably done.)

pure **high cortisol**. dense, real-time, a little chaotic on purpose. watching
the agents coordinate is the demo; the output is almost a side effect of how
fast the thing moves.
