_ghat_ is a mountain pass — the word behind the Western Ghats themselves.
ghatwise is a calm front door to them: treks, rafting, camping, and eco
experiences, booked straight through to verified local operators instead of a
chain of forwarded WhatsApp numbers.

**low cortisol** by design — plan the trip, pay once, show up. verified
operators and real trip management, no chasing a guide for a confirmation
that never comes.

## VITALS

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **turns** — a scattered trip idea → a booked, verified one
- **anti-feature** — no chasing operators for confirmation
