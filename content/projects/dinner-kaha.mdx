_kaha_ means **where**. dinner kaha is the fight that happens in every hostel
group chat, every night, at the exact moment everyone is hungriest and least
willing to decide: where do we eat?

**high cortisol** by design — live hype scores on nearby spots, real-time
check-ins from people already there, and a roulette wheel for when the group
chat can't agree. campus-first, built for MSRIT, social signal instead of a
search bar.

## VITALS

- **squad** — high cortisol // impulse builds
- **turns** — a group-chat stalemate → a decision, fast
- **anti-feature** — no menus to scroll, no reservations
