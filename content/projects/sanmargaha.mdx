_sanmarga_ is **the right path**. sanmargaha is decision guidance with a strong
opinion: you give it the fork you're stuck at, it points, fast. it's built for
the moment where the cost of deliberating is higher than the cost of being
slightly wrong — and it would rather you move.

**high cortisol** by design. confident, quick, allergic to second-guessing. it
won't hand you a balanced 2,000-word essay on your options. it hands you a
direction and a reason, and lets you go.
