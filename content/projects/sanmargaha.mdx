_sanmarga_ is **the right path**. sanmargaha is decision guidance that ends the
loop: you give it the fork you're stuck at, and it points — once, plainly —
so the deliberating stops there.

**low cortisol** by design. it isn't about urgency, it's about relief: one
clear direction instead of an open tab of options, so the decision stops
costing you anything the moment it's made.

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **answers** — a direction, not an essay
- **relief** — the deliberating stops
