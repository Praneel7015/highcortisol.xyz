auralis listens. not in the creepy way — in the way a good note-taker does. it
takes ambient speech, the half-sentences and the sound of a room, and turns it
into something you can act on later: a summary, a reminder, a thread you'd
otherwise have lost.

**low cortisol** by temperament. voice-first interfaces fail when they're loud;
auralis is built to be quiet, to wait, and to only speak when it has something
worth saying. the resting state is the point.

## VITALS

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **input** — ambient speech + sound
- **output** — something quietly useful
