auralis never stops listening. not in the creepy way — in the way a good
note-taker does, except it doesn't get to blink. every half-sentence, every
ambient sound in the room, parsed in real time in case it's the one worth
keeping: a summary, a reminder, a thread you'd otherwise have lost.

**high cortisol** by design. no idle state — voice-first means always-on, a
constant low hum of attention that fires the instant something worth saying
comes through.

## VITALS

- **squad** — high cortisol // intense ships
- **input** — ambient speech + sound, nonstop
- **output** — something worth saying, the moment it lands
