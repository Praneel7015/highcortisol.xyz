nobody reads the terms, including the ones for the challenge this was built
for. clauseclarity reads them for you. paste a lease, a contract, or a terms
page and bedrock (nova) gives back a risk score, a plain-english summary, the
red flags, what you're agreeing to do, and any deadlines buried inside.

built for the aws builder center **deploy your first app** weekend challenge.
**low cortisol**: you read one page instead of forty. the document text is never
stored, only an anonymous counter.
