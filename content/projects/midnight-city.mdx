midnight city is an arcade racer that runs in a browser tab. chase camera, a
long neon circuit, garage paint jobs, and on-screen gas, brake and steer pads
on a phone. ask amazon bedrock (nova) for a livery and it paints the car for
you.

built for the aws builder center **weekend creative challenge**. a racing game
in a weekend is about as **high cortisol** as it sounds.
