future-you-mirror puts you across the table from yourself, ten years out. it's
trained on the shape of your own goals and reflections and answers in a voice
that's recognisably _you_ — just further down the road. calm. reflective. a
little eerie, in the way that talking to yourself always is.

deeply **low cortisol**. it's the slowest software we make: no streaks, no
notifications, no urgency. it just sits with you while you think, and that's the
whole feature.
