future-you-mirror started in may as a mirror you could talk to: you, ten years
out. in october it became a skin mirror. one selfie turns into an aging
timeline, a "with care" portrait beside it, and a few plain next steps: spf
most mornings, a gentle cleanser, fewer sugary drinks. it's not medical advice
and it says so.

rebuilt for the **youcam api skin ai & ecommerce vto hackathon**, using perfect
corp's skin analysis, aging and skin simulation apis. judges get a demo mode
that never sends a photo anywhere.

still deeply **low cortisol**. there are no streaks or notifications, and when
the camera fails you get calm photo advice instead of an error dump.
