zealestate runs the whole lead lifecycle with no human in the loop. a buyer
messages the bot, the agents qualify them, score them, and put a meeting on the
calendar. gemini does the reasoning, cloud run hosts the agents, firestore
keeps the pipeline, and telegram is where the leads talk to it.

built for the **all things agentic hackathon**. 41 commits in nine days, so
**high cortisol**.
