platepilgrim turns dinner into travel. log a dish, stamp its country on an
interactive world map, and take a daily dare from bedrock to cook something
from somewhere you haven't been yet.

built for the aws builder center **full stack challenge**, with cognito sign-in,
cdk and github actions behind it. **low cortisol**: the map fills in slowly,
one meal at a time.
