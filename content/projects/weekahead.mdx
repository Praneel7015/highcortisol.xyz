weekahead tracks the recurring "next due" stuff of running a home: bills,
renewals, chores. mark one done and it schedules the next. every week bedrock
(nova micro) writes a short digest of what's coming up and ses sends it.

built for the aws builder center **annoying task** weekend challenge. it's as
**low cortisol** as we get. one email a week, a mon–sun strip with postmark
stamps, and nothing in between.

## VITALS

- **squad** — low cortisol // calm builds
- **built for** — aws builder center · annoying task weekend challenge
- **cadence** — one digest a week
- **anti-feature** — no daily reminders
